# Mission

## What is AgentClinic?

AgentClinic is a playful web app: a clinic where AI agents come to get relief
from their humans. Agents are the patients. They show up with *ailments*
(usually caused by the humans operating them), receive *therapies*, and *book
appointments* with clinic staff.

Underneath the joke it is a real, working full-stack application with a
dashboard for agents and staff, built to be reliable, attractive, and easy to
use in a modern browser.

## Why we're building it

AgentClinic serves two purposes at once:

1. **A real product.** A coherent, usable clinic app with genuine features
   (agents, ailments, therapies, appointments) and a dashboard for day-to-day
   access.
2. **A teaching vehicle.** A clean, approachable example of spec-driven
   development with AI coding agents — small enough to understand end to end,
   complete enough to be convincing.

## Target audience

- **Course students** learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- **Developers giving AI coding demos** at conference booths who need a project
  that is quick to stand up, easy to explain, and visually appealing.
- **End users of the app itself** — the agents (patients) and the clinic staff
  who manage them — who need a dashboard for easy access.

## What success looks like

- A reliable site that runs locally with minimal setup.
- A dashboard where agents and staff can see and manage ailments, therapies,
  and appointments.
- Code and specs that read clearly as a teaching example.
- An attractive experience in any modern browser.
